KiranaBill for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Developer: Satyadev K

In short

  • Your bills, catalog, prices and customer details are stored on your phone. We do not run a server and do not receive them.
  • When you scan a handwritten chit, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to read the items. That is the only data that leaves your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

Photos and Google Gemini

When you take or choose a photo of a chit, the app resizes it and sends it, with a text instruction, to the Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) over an encrypted connection. Gemini returns the list of items it read. The app does not send anything else with the photo, and it does not send your saved bills, catalog or history.

Anything written on the chit is part of the photo. If a chit shows a customer name or phone number, that text goes to Google along with the rest of the image. Google handles it under its own terms and privacy policy, which depend on the Gemini API plan in use: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms and policies.google.com/privacy.

The app does not keep a copy of the photo after you close the scan, other than what Android keeps for your own camera or gallery.

Data stored on your phone

Bills, catalog items and settings are saved in a private database inside the app. Other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling KiranaBill, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

The app allows Android's standard backup. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your Google backup. That is controlled by your device and Google account settings, not by us.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph a chit. Optional. You can choose a photo from your gallery instead.
  • Internet: to send the chit photo to Google Gemini. Without it, scanning does not work, and you can still add items by hand.

Sharing bills

When you share or print a bill, the app creates a file or message and hands it to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp or a PDF viewer. What that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy. KiranaBill does not send bills anywhere on its own.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We do not show ads or use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not have an account system or a server holding your data.

Your customers' information

If you enter customer names or phone numbers, you are responsible for having a reason to keep them and for using them lawfully. You can edit or delete any saved bill in the app.

Children

KiranaBill is a tool for shop owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Delete individual bills inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Because we hold no copy of your bills, there is nothing to delete on our side. To ask Google about photos sent to Gemini, use the Google links above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, such as adding accounts or cloud backup, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
